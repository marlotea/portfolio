## making the hard things easier

One of the lessons I’ve learned throughout university is to make the difficult things easier for yourself. Sounds funny, if you can make it easier was it really difficult to begin with? Either way, to do so requires some overhead setup and exploring. As James Clear discussed in *Atomic Habits*: motivation is overrated, environment matters more (2018). Our minds have limited capacities, so offload motivation to environment. And if possible, I propose to take it a step further and make it enjoyable for yourself.
